A little bit about me

From Brazil to Sydney Park.

Originally from Brazil, I have a degree in Physical Education and postgraduate qualifications in Nutrition and Fitness. Since moving to Australia I have completed Certificates III and IV and the Diploma in Fitness.

I’ve worked in the fitness industry for 20+ years, most of them exclusively as a personal trainer. Today I train my own clients at Sydney Park and in a private studio in Redfern.

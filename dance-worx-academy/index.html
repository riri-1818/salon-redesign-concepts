      Concert.

      The annual dance concert is the most anticipated event of the year for all of our students. For newcomers to our studio, it’s a spectacular event where every student has the chance to take the stage and showcase their progress.

      Why does it matter? This is a unique opportunity for your child to shine and share their growth with loved ones. It’s their moment to perform in front of family and friends, highlighting all the hard work they’ve put in. A dance concert creates an unforgettable experience, honouring the effort, skill, and success of each dancer.

      Concert tickets

      Tickets to concert shows go on sale online, we will post this on our community pages a few weeks before the concert. We strive to keep concert ticket prices as affordable as possible, though we must account for the significant costs associated with the venues and production. Our concerts are large-scale events featuring cutting-edge lighting, held at top-tier venues that ensure a magical and unforgettable experience for our students, while providing a spectacular and entertaining show for families. Performers do not need a ticket, as they will be supervised backstage during the event.

      Concert levy

      Every performer in any of our end of year concerts will need a Concert Levy. This is $150 that covers the costs associated with running the concert (i.e. Theatre hire, Lighting and sound engineers, prop hire etc.).

      Photography & videography

      We hold photo shoots at the studio so that our students can have professional photos taken in class groups and individually in their awesome costumes. We also organise for professional photography & videography to be taken of the concert. Pricing for these packages will be released closer to the event date.

      Costume hire

      At Dance Worx the students hire the costumes from our in-house costume department. This helps keep costs down and saves parents from spending hundreds of dollars on buying costumes that will end up sitting in a cupboard after being worn once.

      We have introduced a ‘COSTUME LEVY’ which is an “all inclusive” contribution to the costume department and goes towards sourcing new costumes, alterations, mending, wear and tear, cleaning, storing and staff costs.

      We spread the costume hire levy over 40 weeks, which works out to $3.00 per week. Those who enrol into a class after the beginning of the year, will be billed for the costume levy of previous terms to “catch up” on any missed quarterly costume fee payments - so that all students are paying the same for their costumes.

      If your child will not be participating in the concert, please let reception know as early as possible so that you won’t be charged for any future costume hire fees throughout the year.

      No refunds (or exchanges between siblings) can be issued for any costume levies - even if your child cancels their enrolment throughout the year, or is unable to participate in the concert. This is because our costume department works year-round collecting, sorting, sewing, designing etc. Some costumes are purchased or made for the end of year concert as early as February. All of the costume levies go towards the ongoing operating costs of our costume department.

      Ready for the next step?

      We are excited to open new opportunities for our students to experience what it truly means to perform.

      As part of this next chapter, Dance Worx Academy is inviting both current and new students to audition for our Show Troupe, a dedicated pathway designed for dancers ready to transition from recreational classes into focused performance training.

      Our team.

      Founders.

      Roger Mouawad Founder & Director

      Roger Mouawad was born ‘club foot’, and no one suspected that one day, his feet, which were at the time turned 180 degrees inwards, would be dazzling audiences all around the world. For the first six weeks since his birth, he had to wear casts on his feet and then splints until he could walk. Until he was two, he had to wear night shoes to keep correcting his feet. Roger’s dance career started at eleven when, after returning from a soccer game to pick up his sister from a jazz class, he discovered dancing and has been training as a dancer since.

      Roger’s accolades and talents took him over all corners of the globe from corporate shows, to live variety concerts, full scholarships and employment as a professional artist with such notable companies as Paris Opera Ballet, Atlantic City Ballet and Contemporary Company - where he performed in ballets such as A Midsummer Night’s Dream, Dracula and The Nutcracker as a soloist and in pas de deux roles - and Carnival Cruise Lines, just to name a few. His training and dedication prepared him as a finalist at the Youth America Grand Prix (New York) in 2008 and 2009, he was the semi-finalist at the 2008 Genee International Ballet competition in Auckland, New Zealand, as well as at the Tanzolymp Ballet Competition in Berlin, Germany in 2009 and semi-finalist at the McDonald’s Performing Arts Challenge 2008 Ballet Scholarship in Sydney, Australia, as well as many others.

      Roger’s training, like his achievements in the dance world, are equally extensive, from completing the FATD’s in Tap and Jazz with Honours Gold Star, to training with Hilary Kaplan at Alegria and completing RAD Ballet exams with Distinction, being awarded a full scholarship to study a diploma of dance at Escola de Dança do Conservatório Nacional, Lisbon, Portugal (Portuguese National Conservatory and School of Dance) and most recently graduating with Distinction in a Bachelor of Dance degree from the Academy of Music and Performing Arts (AMPA) in Sydney, Australia.

      While his achievements are stellar and well-deserved, Roger’s success is a combination of hard-work, passion, and sacrifice seasoned with talent and charisma. As a young man who grew in Sydney’s West, near Parramatta, Roger understands that dance can help young people develop discipline and stamina to work for their dreams, no matter the field or interest.

      “Dance relaxes my mind and helps me express what cannot be spoken. In dance, I find freedom and strength and confidence. It keeps me alive and it is this passion I want to pass on to the next generation so that they can see that you can express yourself through movement, not punches and that any obstacle can be tackled with discipline and focus: all qualities that I learnt from dance.”

From studying in a performing arts school that’s where she found her passion for music altogether. This is where her journey began!

Upon completion, Sheridan set her sights high jet setting all the way to the concrete jungle, New York City! Gaining her credentials and professional theatre training at AMDA NYC, ‘The American Musical and Dramatic Academy’. She then jumped back on another plane taking her across Europe sitting down on London’s West End with the Classic Musical ‘West Side Story’!

      Policies.

      Changes to enrolment

      Students may choose to change or “swap” classes. When SWAPPING classes but staying on the same “pricing package” (ie. number of classes attending), the billing stays the same.

      If adding a class and moving to a higher pricing package, you will need to complete the form so we can adjust pricing and classes.

      If reducing your child’s enrolment and moving to a lower pricing package, a form will need to be filled out so that the remaining payments reflect the reduced enrolment. Please be aware that costume fees and performance levies are not refundable. If your child is not participating in the concert, please notify reception at the beginning of the year so that your child’s billing can be adjusted in the system.

      If cancelling your enrolment completely, and withdrawing as a student, the parent MUST complete a cancellation form. Unless we are notified, billing will continue automatically even if the student is not attending, and refunds for missed classes are not available under any circumstances.

      Cancellation can only occur during the school holidays and once the term resumes, students will be locked in. Students are encouraged to continue in their classes for the term weeks, alternatively the fees for the term are forfeited.

      Submit a change to enrolment
      Missed class policy

      If a student has to miss a class because they are sick, we unfortunately do not offer make up lessons.

      In the case of significant injury where a student is unable to dance for longer than 4 weeks, we can issue a credit for those weeks’ fees. (On direct debit this will mean that automatic payments stop early at the end of the year). This applies ONLY if we are notified in advance by writing, and with a medical certificate, with details of the number of weeks the student will miss.

      We cannot provide credit or discount for classes missed due to extended family holidays.

      Terms and conditions

      (as agreed to on the enrolment forms) Full 10-week term payment is billed before the beginning of each term, and is due before the term commences, unless on a direct debit plan (with a form filled out at the studio) which will spread the payments throughout the year. Enrolment rolls over from term to term, until the end of the calendar year, unless cancelled with the form below. Once each term has commenced, parents are liable for the full term of fees, whether classes are attended or not. It is the parents’ responsibility to officially cancel the enrolment prior to commencement of term. If cancelling mid-term, no refund is available for the remaining weeks. We highly recommend waiting until the end of the term to drop number of classes, or cancel enrolment. Registration fee and dance fees paid are not refundable - unless within the first 2 weeks of enrolment of a new student, as part of our 2-week satisfaction guarantee, however all enrolled classes over both weeks must be attended by the student to be eligible for this refund. Any student (including Troupe members) enrolled in recreational classes that require costume levies will be liable for the full costume levy for any term that the student is enrolled in the class.

      NSW Active Kids and Creative Kids vouchers

      The NSW Government is helping make dance and performing arts more affordable for families by providing Active Kids and Creative Kids vouchers. Dance Worx Academy is an official provider. For effective management of this scheme, we require voucher codes to be lodged via the online form.

      Dance Worx Academy will ONLY credit your account once we have received reimbursement for this voucher from Service NSW. The length of this process is outside of our control - their systems take a few weeks. The credit is applied to your Dance Worx Academy statement.

      Vouchers cannot be redeemed for cash, either fully or partially and is only redeemable while this child is enrolled at Dance Worx Academy. Voucher value is strictly not refundable as per the terms of conditions set by the NSW government.

      For more information about the vouchers you can visit https://www.service.nsw.gov.au

      Code of conduct

      At Dance Worx Academy, we are committed to fostering a positive, respectful, and inclusive environment where students, parents, and staff work together to create a supportive community. To ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for everyone, we ask all participants to adhere to the following code of conduct:

      1. Respect for Others: Treat all students, instructors, staff, and visitors with kindness and respect. Encourage positive interactions and teamwork among peers. Maintain an inclusive atmosphere where everyone feels welcome, regardless of skill level, background, or experience.
      2. Punctuality and Attendance: Arrive on time for all classes, rehearsals, and performances. Notify the studio in advance if you are unable to attend a class. Consistent attendance is vital to progress and team success.
      3. Dress Code: Wear the appropriate attire for each class, as instructed by the dance studio. Proper footwear must be worn for safety reasons, and hair should be secured in a neat style. Keep personal belongings organized and avoid bringing unnecessary items into the studio.
      4. Behaviour and Attitude: Show a positive attitude and be open to learning and constructive feedback. Follow directions and guidelines set by instructors at all times. Avoid disruptive behaviour, including talking, excessive noise, or distracting others during class.
      5. Safety First: Prioritize safety by warming up before classes, stretching, and using proper techniques. Report any injuries or concerns to the instructor immediately. Be mindful of your surroundings, and respect the studio’s equipment and space.
      6. Communication: Keep open lines of communication with your instructor regarding any challenges or special needs. Parents should keep the studio informed of any changes in contact information or emergency details. Address any concerns or issues in a respectful and professional manner.
      7. Respect for the Studio Space: Treat the studio facilities and equipment with care and respect. Clean up after yourself and avoid leaving personal items behind. Maintain the cleanliness and tidiness of the studio at all times.
      8. No Bullying or Harassment: Any form of bullying, harassment, or discrimination will not be tolerated. Foster an environment where everyone feels safe, valued, and supported.
      9. Performance Etiquette: Perform with respect for fellow dancers and the audience. Refrain from unnecessary behaviour backstage during performances and rehearsals. Be courteous to other performers and respect the stage as a shared space.
      10. Parent and Guardian Responsibilities: Parents should support the studio’s mission and values and reinforce positive behaviour in students. Attend parent meetings and stay informed about studio events, policies, and schedules. Maintain appropriate behavior and respect the space during observation times.

      Our beautiful but busy premises are cleaned daily. Please respect our studios and help us to keep them looking nice. Keep your feet off the furniture and hands off the walls and mirrors. Take all rubbish with you or place it in one of the many bins provided. Drink spillages and “accidents” are parents’ responsibility to clean up immediately, please ask at reception for a mop or sponge if required.

      Parents and students and siblings are asked to keep all noise to a minimum when in the foyer or waiting in between classes. Our studios are soundproofed, but some noise still leaks through and can distract the classes still in progress.

      By following this Code of Conduct, we ensure that Dance Worx Academy remains a place where every dancer can thrive, grow, and enjoy their dance experience. We appreciate your cooperation and commitment to maintaining a positive and respectful environment!

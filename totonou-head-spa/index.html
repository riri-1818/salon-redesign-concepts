    脱毛Hair removal

    SHR hair removal. Choose the areas, see the total.

    SHR hair removal, an abbreviation for “Super Hair Removal”, is a type of “heat storage hair removal” that repeatedly exposes hair follicles to weak light while storing heat. It damages the “bulge area” where hair is produced, allowing hair removal regardless of the hair growth cycle, making it highly effective on sunburned skin, fine hair, and gray hair, and is characterized by its ability to shorten treatment times and duration.

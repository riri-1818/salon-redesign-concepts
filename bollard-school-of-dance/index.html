Performing

For dancers who love the stage.

Concerts

The end-of-year concert is a highlight for every student and teacher, and all classes take part in a half-yearly concert in Term 2.

Troupes & competitions

Performance and competition troupes for dedicated dancers, with championships and solo competitions through the year.

Examinations

Classical ballet, tap, jazz and hip hop exams through the Australian Teachers of Dancing. They are optional, but students are encouraged to take part.

FAQ

Before the first class.

Do you offer trial lessons?

Yes! All new students receive their first week of lessons free. Contact us to book your free trial.

What does my dancer wear to their first lesson?

Comfortable clothing such as tights, shorts and t-shirts. Bare feet is always best.

How long are the dance lessons?

Our lessons vary from 30 to 45 minutes.

Do you accept Active & Creative Kids vouchers?

Yes! We are an approved Active & Creative Kids provider.

How do I pay for my lessons?

Classes are invoiced each term, and invoices are sent to your nominated email address.

Are dancers required to wear a uniform?

Students are asked to wear the studio uniform. It lets teachers see posture and alignment, and creates a sense of belonging. Uniform and dance shoes can be bought at the studio.

Is there an end of year concert?

Yes! Our concert is a yearly highlight. All classes also take part in a half-yearly concert in Term 2.

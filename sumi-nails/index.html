Ladiposh

Ladiposh is a soft gel nail system from Japan. It’s a breathable, long-lasting material and one we recommend to our clients.

Ladiposh allows for unlimited design and nail art, but most importantly it is healthier for the nail.

It is safe and easy to remove and does not require the use of UV.

Courses

  1. Hand Care3 hours$360

    This lesson is the perfect way to learn all about the basics. For those interested, we cover what you can expect in becoming a nail technician. All students must complete this module before moving on to the others.

    Areas coveredSanitation + Hygiene · Shaping · Buffing · Wet Cuticle Care · Hand Massages · Polish Application

  2. Basic Nail Art with Polish2.5 hours$300

    In this lesson, you will learn basic Nail Art using polish materials. The skills you learn can also be applied to gel or acrylic materials.

    Areas coveredFrench Manicure · Paint Art · Marble Art · Stones + Decorations · Hologram

  3. The Acrylic Course7.5 hours$900

    This set of 4 modules/lessons span 7.5 hours. Learning how to work with acrylic will expand your range of skills and services.

Your personal trainer

This is a space created by Rasha.

I have been in the fitness industry since 2006, and have worked as a personal trainer and class instructor in a few Sydney gyms. I created this small studio to give my clients quality equipment and a training style that is safe, effective and enjoyable.

It’s a space where no one feels intimidated, and anyone can enjoy being in the weights room and feel confident. A place to sweat, to work hard and to have fun.

My focus is to help you move better and to get stronger.

    Who we are

    Our journey

    Knock Out Parkinson’s Disease (KOPD) was conceived from a personal journey with Parkinson’s and a passion for combat sports. Adrian Unger, a boxing enthusiast diagnosed with Parkinson’s, discovered the profound benefits of high-intensity exercise for managing his condition.

    Adrian then joined forces with Dylan Resnekov, a world champion Muay Thai fighter, and Nate, an exercise physiologist and kickboxer, to create KOPD.

    The resulting program integrates the intensity and discipline of fight sports with the evidence-based approaches of exercise physiology to offer a powerful and targeted intervention for individuals with Parkinson’s.

    Our core values

    Community

    We are community first, always. We ride the highs and lows together, when one suffers we all struggle together, and when one wins we celebrate as one.

    Beyond the training floor, we create space for connection through shared moments like coffee and lunch, building a compassionate and personal environment grounded in genuine camaraderie.

    No one faces Parkinson’s alone here, everyone is supported, seen, and part of the family.

    Authenticity

    Our approach is deeply personal and individually designed from the moment someone walks through the door.

    From initial conversations to tailored classes and ongoing follow up, everything we do is built around the individual.

    Guided by integrity and evidence, we deliver high quality, safe, and thoughtful programs that are as unique as the people we serve.

    Progress with purpose

    We believe in showing up each day with the desire to be better and bringing whatever energy and capacity you have.

    Parkinson’s is a hard and relentless disease and we meet it with a fighting spirit that is pragmatic, intentional, and focused on what truly matters.

    By prioritising progress over perfection, we support meaningful and consistent improvement that empowers people to keep moving forward.

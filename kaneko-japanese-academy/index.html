    共進

    Kyōshin Learning Method

    Two well-matched students, moving forward together.

    Kyōshin (共進) lessons pair students of a similar level who are compatible study partners. Each still gets one-on-one attention from a native Japanese-speaking tutor. Students share work, ideas and perspectives, and learn from each other’s mistakes as well as their own.

    Available to Year 11 and Year 12 students, and only to select students on a case-by-case basis.

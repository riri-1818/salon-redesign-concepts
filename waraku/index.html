    Opening hours
    Open daily from 9:30am to 6:00pm.
    Bookings
    By phone or text message, in English or Japanese. Text messages are answered during business hours only.
    Health fund rebates
    All of our therapists are recognised providers with most private health funds. HICAPS is available, so you can claim your rebate on the spot. If your fund is not registered with HICAPS, we can issue a receipt.
    Cancellations
    Please give at least 24 hours’ notice. Otherwise a full cancellation fee may apply.

    Gift vouchers
    Gift vouchers are available for birthdays, weddings, anniversaries and other occasions.

    Good to know

    Before you come.

    Hours
    Open daily from 9:30am to 6:00pm. Bookings by phone or text message. Text messages are responded to during business hours only.
    Health fund rebates
    All of our therapists are recognised providers with most private health funds. HICAPS is available so that you can claim your rebate instantly. If your fund is not registered with HICAPS, we are able to issue you a receipt.
    Cancellations
    A minimum of 24 hours’ notice is required. Otherwise a full cancellation fee may apply.
    Parking
    Westfield Shopping Centre, directly opposite Lemon Grove, offers up to 2 free hours of parking. The most convenient entrance for WARAKU is on Level 4.
    Gift vouchers
    Gift vouchers are available, for birthdays, weddings, anniversaries, special occasions, workplace incentives and appreciation.

yokototalbeauty TreatCard Loyalty Program

At yokototalbeauty, we are excited to offer our clients the Phorest TreatCard loyalty program, a unique system designed to reward you for your loyalty and enhance your overall experience. The TreatCard program is fully integrated with our booking system and app, allowing you to earn points every time you make a purchase.

How It Works:

  • Earn Points: Earn loyalty points for every dollar you spend on treatments and products at yokototalbeauty. Your points will accumulate automatically and can be tracked easily through the yokototalbeauty app.
  • Redeem Treats: Once you’ve earned enough points, you can redeem them for special treats. Unlike traditional discounts, the TreatCard rewards you with services and products you might not have tried before, introducing you to new and exciting offerings.
  • Flexible Rewards: You can use your points for a variety of rewards, such as a complimentary eyebrow treatment, a new product, or an exclusive PMU procedure. Our program is designed to ensure that your loyalty is celebrated in a meaningful way.

No Expiry:

Your loyalty points never expire, giving you the flexibility to redeem them whenever it suits you best.

How to Redeem:

  • Log into your yokototalbeauty app.
  • Go to the TreatCard section to view your points balance.
  • Choose the treat you want to redeem your points for.
  • Inform the front desk or your therapist about the treat you wish to redeem during your visit.
  • The staff will process the redemption and apply it to your service or product.

Join the yokototalbeauty TreatCard loyalty program today and start enjoying the benefits of being a valued client. We look forward to surprising and delighting you with every visit!

Mobile Apps

Download the yokototalbeauty app to manage your appointments, track your TreatCard points, and redeem rewards conveniently.

About Yoko

“To Yoko, this isn’t a kind of work but fun.”

Led by master tattoo artist Yoko Hamaguchi (Tokyo, Japan), who brings years of experience in both individualised treatments and training. She enjoys seeing her clients’ joyful expression when the work is complete.

  • Eyelash: certified by Tia Rouge in Sydney
  • Eyebrow advanced: certified by Tanya Beauty Care in Sydney
  • Eyeliner, lip and eyebrow advanced: certified by Dmitry Khatkin
  • Scalp Micro-Pigmentation (SMP): certified by Scalp Culture

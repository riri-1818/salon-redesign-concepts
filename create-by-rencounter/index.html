      Policies

      The Japanese text is a reference translation. Please refer to the English text for the official wording.

      Refund policy: services (hair salon and head spa)

      At CREATE by ReNCOUNTER, we strive for absolute satisfaction across our services and natural product lines.

      Service satisfaction: We do not offer cash refunds on completed hair or head spa treatments. However, if you are not entirely satisfied with your result, please contact us within 7 days of your appointment. We will gladly invite you back to discuss an adjustment session with a stylist.

      Cancellation policy: Less than 24 hours’ notice will result in a charge equivalent to 50% of the reserved service amount. A “no show” will be charged 100% of the reserved amount. If there is no response after 3 days, we will terminate your appointment. So please contact us to make changes to your appointment to avoid the fee.

      Refund policy: products

      Change of mind: Due to hygiene and safety standards regarding natural beauty products, we cannot accept returns or offer refunds for opened or used lifestyle products.

      Damaged or faulty items: If your product arrives damaged or is faulty, please contact us at our store email within 14 days of purchase with photographic proof. We will arrange a replacement or partial/full refund depending on the damage.

      Privacy policy

      CREATE by ReNCOUNTER is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) under the Privacy Act 1988.

      Information we collect. Personal details: name, email address, phone number, and delivery address when you purchase products or book a salon session. Health data: voluntary information regarding your hair history, scalp health, or ingredient allergies to ensure safe spa and salon treatments. Payment information: all online transaction payments are securely processed via encrypted third-party gateways (e.g., Stripe, PayPal). We do not store your credit card details on our servers.

      How we use your data. To fulfill product orders, manage your appointment calendar, and send booking reminders. With your consent, to send occasional email updates and social media communication regarding new natural beauty product arrivals or seasonal salon promotions. You can opt out at any time.

      Accessibility statement

      CREATE by ReNCOUNTER is dedicated to ensuring digital accessibility for all visitors, including individuals with disabilities. We are continually improving the user experience by applying relevant Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.1 AA) standards.

      Features we maintain. Clean structural hierarchy: utilizing proper heading structures and layouts to assist screen readers. Alt text: applying descriptive text to core functional images across the site. Contrast and readability: ensuring text elements contrast cleanly against backdrops for visually impaired users.

      Feedback and contact. If you experience any difficulty accessing elements of our website while exploring our hair services or natural products, please let us know. We welcome your feedback and will work swiftly to resolve any digital barriers. Email: rencounter2012@gmail.com

Sunrise and smile.

‘Akala’ means sunrise and smile in Hawaiian heritage.

At our salon in the middle of Sydney at Ultimo, you will experience the most ethical Aveda plant-based products and our selected trendy Japanese products. We offer a huge range of hairdressing services, including balayage and Japanese straightening.

Services & price

Beautiful hair results.

Highly experienced team of international hairdressers.

The unique environment we offer in our hair salon allows you to relax while your winning & highly experienced team of international hairdressers ensures you the result you want.

We specialize in maintaining the integrity of your hair using the globally acclaimed, highest quality products made with all natural ingredients such as Aveda hair product and other Japanese products, and provide you with Japanese hair relaxing and straightening services as well as scalp and hair treatments. Stress-relieving scalp massage using oil, warm, aromatic hot towel, neck and shoulders, hand massages.

Each team member has the knowledge and skill to give beautiful results and will provide you with thorough guidance to feel comfortable using it at home on your own.

Prices for

Additional charges

  • Long hair fee: $10–$30, depending on hair length.
  • Wash & blow dry: $20 (applies to services that don’t include a cut).
  • MARBB microbubbles: included with all services.

Salon policies

  • All colour and perm prices are base rates and are subject to change according to hair thickness and length. Prices do not include gloss service.
  • Shampoo & dry $20.
  • All colour and perm, treatments charge a service fee of $20 for wash and finishing dry.
  • Running more than 15 minutes late to your appointment may compromise your original booking or reschedule.
  • Student discount: for high school students with presentation of Australian student ID there is a 10% discount with all the service except retail products.
  • Confirming appointments, cancelling or rescheduling: 72 hours notice to cancel your booking and a full refund will be transferred back to your nominated bank account. 48 hours notice when rescheduling an appointment so that he or she can fill that appointment. We reserve the right to charge a cancellation fee on any appointments cancelled with less than 48 hours notice / no show, and future appointments will only be accepted with the payment of a deposit only.

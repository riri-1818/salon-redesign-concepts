Health Fund Benefits:

Some health funds provide eligible participants with the ability to claim back a portion of the cost of their learn to swim lessons, provided these lessons are undertaken at an AUSTSWIM Recognised Swim Centre that also meets individual health funds’ recognition requirements and terms and conditions.

Fun Fitness Swim School is an AUSTSWIM Recognised centre that has met the recognition requirements, so if you are a member of a healthfund that offers this, then please see below for more information on how you can potentially claim benefits from one of the health funds shown.

HCF is Australia’s largest not-for-profit health fund, providing member benefits towards a range of Health Management programs. One of these programs is Learn to swim, for lessons conducted by HCF recognised swim schools holding AUSTSWIM Recognition. (Squad and recreational swimming are excluded).

You can claim benefits for this program if you have an eligible level of cover and have served your six month waiting period. Check with HCF for details by calling 13 13 34.

Please quote Fun Fitness Swim School, as per issued invoice.

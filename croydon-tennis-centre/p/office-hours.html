Croydon Tennis Centre is open daily from 7am till 11pm.

Bookings can be made online at any time. Online Court Bookings must be made before 5pm for evening play. Evening Court Bookings are only available before dark or when staff are present onsite. Any online bookings made after this time for the same evening will not be honoured. Racquet hire is not available for online bookings unless previously arranged.

Monday to Friday: 9:30am to 7:30pm Saturday & Sunday: 8:00am to 3:00pm Public Holidays: Closed

Office Hours can change without notice due to adverse weather conditions. If the weather looks uncertain you must call the centre before making your booking or to check your existing booking on wet days. If courts are unplayable, the Centre will do its best to contact players using SMS details provided.

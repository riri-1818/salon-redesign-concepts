Facilities & Tennis Courts

Floodlit synthetic grass courts

Croydon Tennis Centre offers one of the finest tennis club facilities in the inner west, with four quality, well drained synthetic grass tennis courts, each court with individual floodlights.

Croydon Tennis Centre is located in a picturesque setting in the heart of Croydon in Sydney.

Open Early to 11pm 7 days

Vacation Care @ CTC OOSH

Croydon Tennis Centre OOSH is offering Vacation Care during all school holidays throughout the year. Our Vacation Care programs encourage children to be involved in a variety of fun and exciting activities, with access to onsite large open grassed areas and adjacent local parkland.

Our fun and engaging program have something to keep your child busy during the school holidays!

Full Day Program 7am - 7pm includes the following:-

  • Breakfast, morning tea, lunch & afternoon tea
  • Inclusion into daily funfitness program run by our qualified P.E teacher
  • Supervised swim for children who are competent swimmers
  • Activities as per the Vacation Care Program
  • Access to a variety of age appropriate activities both indoor & outdoor
  • Dinner option available at 5:30pm

$141.00 - $177.14 depending on daily program (Pre-Booked Dinner $9)

You may not need the full day program so you are welcome to mix and match which parts of the day you will need care:-

  • Before Care 7am - 9am (breakfast included) $16.80
  • Short Day Program 9am-3pm (morning tea & lunch included) $109.00 - $147.00
  • After Care 3pm - 7pm (afternoon tea included) $39.00 (pre-booked dinner $9)

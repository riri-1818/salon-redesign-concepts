Active Kids Vouchers

Croydon Tennis Centre is accepting NSW Active Kids $100 Sports Vouchers that can be used toward the cost of any of our term based Tennis or Swimming lessons for kids.

The voucher can be used at any time during the calendar year it was issued. For further information or to apply for an Active kids voucher, click here.

If you would like to use an Active Kids Voucher for your childs Tennis or Swimming please email or print out the voucher and present to the office.

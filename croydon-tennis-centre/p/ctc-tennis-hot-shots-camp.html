CTC Tennis Hot Shots Camp

Our #School Holiday #Tennis Hot Shots Tennis Camps are run every school holidays. The format of our School Holiday Tennis and Fitness Camps is based on the CTC Tennis Hot Shots junior development program and delivery methodology. Using modified equipment and scaled down sizes of the court ensures optimum development and participation for all children as well as the enjoyment of all participants regardless of the age or standard. This is our most popular School Holiday Activity Program.

  • Full day Tennis & Fitness Camp
  • Duration: 9AM - 3PM
  • Ages: 5 - 16 (must have started primary school if aged 5)
  • Sausage Sizzle provided daily

Typical Day Outline:

9.00 - 9.30: Warm Up

9.30 - 9.40: Separated into groups

9.40 - 10.00: Tennis warming up

10.00 - 10.30: Skill development

10.30 - 11.00: Morning tea break

11.00 - 12.30: Skill development

12.30 - 1.30: Lunch break

1.30 - 2.30: Skill development

2.30 - 3.00: Supervised match play and games

Cost: $385 / 5 day week or $80 / day

Croydon Tennis centre has four synthetic grass floodlit tennis courts for hire and comprehensive professional tennis coaching services, catering for tennis enthusiasts of all ages.

Croydon Tennis Centre is a market leader in delivering the ANZ Tennis Hots Shots Junior Development program & FitBit Cardio Tennis for everyone.

Our Professional Tennis coaching consists of a variety of services ranging from children as young as 3 commencing PREP Gross Motor Skills Classes, Junior Group Classes through Private Lessons to Adult Classes and structured Competition and Social Tennis, known as Tennis Anyone?.

School Holiday programs are a must for children and include our ANZ Tennis Hot Shots Camps, our Multi Sport Camps and our NEW Sports Academic Camps.

Since our alignment with Tennis Australia in January 2010, we have implemented the ANZ Tennis Hot Shots Junior Development program which is recognised by the International Tennis Federation (ITF) as the ideal pathway for player development.

Croydon Tennis Centre in alliance with Funfitness provides outdoor fitness services along with a highly respected Learn to Swim Program in our Indoor Saltwater Heated Pool. Classes are taught for Mum's & Bubs from the age of 4 months through to Adults.

Whether you are a casual player or a serious regular player or just wanting to get fit, we have something to suit everyone.

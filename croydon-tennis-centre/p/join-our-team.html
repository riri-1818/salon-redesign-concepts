OOSH Educator Positions available Casual and Part-time

The Role

We are looking to appoint casual and part-time OOSH Educators to work in our before, after school care and vacation care OOSH service. We are located at Croydon tennis Centre , giving us access to unique grounds for children to enjoy and explore!

We are looking for staff who are Friendly, Fun, confident, creative and great with children. You will work alongside enthusiastic and motivated staff who are dedicated to working with school aged children and providing new, unique and exciting programs for them.

About you

  • Essential requirements for this position are:New working with children check
  • A current first-aid certificate and asthma and anaphylaxis training or willingness to undertake training in this area
  • The ability to interact well with children and build positive relationships with families
  • Ability to work and contribute to a teamGreat communication skills
  • Enthusiastic and hardworking

Desirable but not Essential

  • Centre based experience within the child care industry
  • Knowledge of National Quality and My Time Our Place Frameworks
  • Child Protection Training
  • Qualifications in Children's Education or equivalent
  • Expertise in a specific child interest subject such as coding, tutoring, dance, drama, music, etc...

Our Mission

Our aim is to make Croydon Tennis Centre and Fun Fitness a one stop destination for the broader community. We are committed to delivering the highest quality tennis coaching, fitness training and swimming lessons available in Sydney, in a safe, friendly, family oriented environment at Croydon Tennis Centre.

Our mission is to be the preferred choice by families for fun, tennis and fitness. Our family owned business is committed to providing “Good Old Fashioned” service combined with modern facilities.

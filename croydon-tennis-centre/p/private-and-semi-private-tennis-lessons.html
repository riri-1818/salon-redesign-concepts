Private and Semi Private Tennis Lessons

Private lessons are available for both children and adults of all ages and are ideally suited for those preferring an individual approach.

Private lessons are great for people learning tennis for the first time providing a long term one-on-one coaching committment and lessons are tailored to your individual needs OR for the player wanting to work on a specific technical or tactical aspect of the game.

There are two options; Weekly payments, or Advance payment for the term in a lesson pack. You can take advantage of Lesson Packs, only available to our members. We guarantee your full number of lessons. In the rare event your lesson is washed out, we find a suitable alternative time for a make-up lesson.

All Croydon Tennis Centre coaches are fully qualified and accredited.

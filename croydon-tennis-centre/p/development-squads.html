Giants 12 + years old

The Develop program consists of two 60 minute weekly group lessons or one 90 minute weekly development squad. It is targetted at juniors who have expressed a desire to play more tennis and are on the way to making tennis their favourite sport.

The Develop lesson format will essentially re-enforce the fundamentals and assist in the rate of development advancement.

Each childs development rate is dependent upon their level of commitment and individual capability as well as biological development. Some children simply progress faster, some take a little more time however if the right skills are developed and worked on at the right time, each child has the potential to succeed given the right amount of effort.

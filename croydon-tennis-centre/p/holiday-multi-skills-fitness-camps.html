Holiday Multi Skills & Fitness Camps

Our Holiday #Multi-skills Program is designed for those wanting to try a range of sports and activities allowing children to have fun, learn and play. This quality multi-skills program provides a range of activities for children of all abilities.

Sports Included:

Soccer, Football, Cricket, Swimming, Tennis, T-Ball, Frisbee, Volleyball, Dodge Ball and much more.

  • Full day #Multi-Skills Camp
  • Duration: 9AM - 3PM
  • Ages: 5 - 16 (must be attending school)
  • Sausage Sizzle provided daily
  • Recreational swim in our heated salt water pool for competent swimmers

Cost: $385 / 5 day week or $80 / day

Benefits of the camp:

  • Exposure to variety of Multi Skills Games & Sports
  • Low coach per students ratio
  • All Coaches are qualified
  • Development of new Skills
  • Free Sausage Sizzle Daily

Typical day outline is:

  • 9 - 9.30 am: Warm Up
  • 9.30 - 10.30 am: Sport 1 - Skill Development
  • 10.30 - 11 am: Morning Tea
  • 11 - 12.30 pm: Sport 1 - Games
  • 12.30 - 1 pm: Lunch
  • 1 - 2 pm: Sport 2 - Skill Development
  • 2 - 3 pm: Sport 2 - Games

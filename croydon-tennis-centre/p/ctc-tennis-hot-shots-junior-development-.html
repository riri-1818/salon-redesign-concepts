CTC Tennis Hot Shots Junior Development Camp

Our CTC Hot Shots Junior Development Holiday program is for young players who have mastered the basics of tennis and is aimed at providing development advancement through quality performance coaching amongst committed passionate piayers.

This is the camp for Orange & Green Ball players

If your child(ren) are looking for a strong experience in quality performance coaching, the 5 hours of tennis in the CTC Hot Shots Junior Development camp is ideal.

With a focus on technical and tactical development, your child(ren) will be able to improve specific areas of their game. With monitored match play they will be able to reproduce and apply all skills learnt during the morning session and receive valuable feedback. This camp is suitable for those who would like to take their tennis to the next level of competition.

Typical Day Outline:

9.00 - 9.30: Warm Up

9.30 - 9.40: Dispatched into groups

9.40 - 10.00: Tennis warming up

10.00 - 11.00: Technical skill development

11.00 - 11.30: Morning tea break

11.30 - 12.30: Tactical link to technical development drill

12.30 - 1.15: Lunch break

1.15 - 3.00: Supervised mentored match play

Cost: $430 / 5 day week or $95 / day

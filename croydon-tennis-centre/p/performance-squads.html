High Performance Squad

The Performance Squad program is by INVITATION ONLY. It consists of 2 - 3 weekly tennis lessons and an additional session focused on movement and optimal fitness development. Only participants who expressed more interest in tennis and are taking part in the Develop program may be admitted to this program

The main difference between Performance and Development is the session duration and intesity of the progressions. INVITATION ONLY guarantees the highest level of all participants.

The rate of progress is still dependent on each participants individual capability as well as biological development. However ALL participants in the Perform program should possess basic fundamentals and the advancement is mainly sped up by hard work and higher participation in competitions.

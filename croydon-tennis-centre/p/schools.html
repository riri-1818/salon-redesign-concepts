Schools

Croydon Tennis Centre delivers Tennis Hot Shots to primary schools three ways. 1. Tennis in Schools program Croydon Tennis Centre delivers Tennis Hot Shots each school term to children who wish to receive coaching during school hours. 2. Active After-School Communities (AASC) program Croydon Tennis Centre delivers Tennis Hot Shots each term for seven weeks in conjunction with the Australian Sports Commissions (government funded) Active After-School Communities program. 3. National Schools Partnership program(NSPP) A program which was launched in October 2012 by Tennis Australia. Schools who adopt this program and have Croydon Tennis Centre deliver Tennis Hot Shots for a 4-6 week period receive a $1,500 starter pack. If you're school is interested in discussing incorporating Tennis Hot Shots, please contact Sam at Croydon Tennis Centre on 9747 4259.

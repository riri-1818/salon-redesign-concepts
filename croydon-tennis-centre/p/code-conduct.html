Players must follow staff directions at all times. Aggressive behaviour towards staff or other players will not be tolerated. See Tennis Court Rules.

We reserve the right to refuse service to anyone for any reason at any time.

We are a device free enviroment, all mobile devices and electronics must be handed in at sign-in and will be returned at sign-out.

CTC has a zero tollerance for racism, reverse racism, bullying, harrasment, physical and/or verbal agression, and disrespect to any member of staff and other children.

Cancellation of enrolment will occur without refund if your child(ren) violate(s) the CTC’s code of conduct.

No Smoking

In the interests of Public Health and comfort, smoking is not permitted on the courts, in the changing rooms or within the administrative areas of Croydon Tennis Centre.

Parking

Off street parking is available. We ask all patrons when parking to be considerate and take up one car space only. As there are children in close proximity, a speed limit of less than 5km per hour is enforced.

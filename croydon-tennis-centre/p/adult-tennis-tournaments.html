Adult Tennis Event : Doubles Challenge

The Adult Doubles Challenge is open to all players with the ability to serve and rally. All players will be sorted depending on their ability. Doubles only. You can bring your own partner or just come along and be teamed up with a partner of similar ability.

The Tournament will be organised in a Round Robin format.

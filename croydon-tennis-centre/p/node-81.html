Croydon Tennis Centre 43 Brighton Street CROYDON NSW 2132

Croydon Tennis Centre is centrally located, just off Liverpool Road and a 5 min walk from Croydon Train Station. To find out which bus or train will get you to our centre, please call the Transport Info Line on 131 500 or visit the Transport Info line website.

Access to the tennis centre is off Brighton Street between houses 43 and 45.

Off street parking is available.

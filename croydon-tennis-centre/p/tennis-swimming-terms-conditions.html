Tennis Anyone - Social Tennis

Playing at the Wrong Level

The centre reserves the right to advise players of their appropriate level/standard which overrides a player's self-assessment. On rare occasions players can grade themselves incorrectly - usually too high - and a coach or staff member will advise them to which level class or social tennis group they can participate in.

If a staff member advises you that your level is not appropriate for a particular Class/Social Group we ask players not to take offence and dispute the assessment. The centre expects Members/Players to follow and respect the advice of the coach/staff in an appropriate way.

Once players have been advised not to participate at a certain level, they cannot book into that class or group until such a time as they reach that level through coaching and regular practice - the staff will advise when a player is ready to move up.

Players who continue to re-book into levels where they have been advised otherwise will be refused entry into that level.

Centre players may request a grading session with a centre coach or the Director to verify their level if they feel it is too low.

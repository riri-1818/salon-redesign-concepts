CCS

Our centre is approved for CCS, all eligible families will be able to claim CCS from the date care commences. For information on how to claim, please use this link:-https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/child-care-subsidy?utm_id=10

Cessation of Care

Services Australia generally won't pay CCS for any absences before a child physically attends or after the last day your child physically attends care.

Therefore for CCS to apply to the first day booked for your child’s care, your child must attend; and your child must attend on the last day of care or you will be charged full fees otherwise.

For further information see:- When you can use absence days before your child attends care or after their last day of attendance https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/child-care-subsidy-if-your-child-absent-from-child-care?context=41186

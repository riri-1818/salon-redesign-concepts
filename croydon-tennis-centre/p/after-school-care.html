Before & After School Care @ CTC OOSH

Croydon Tennis Centre OOSH is up and running and we are servicing local schools in the area for Before and After School Care.

We offer a drop off and pick up service to and from school as well as all meals being included such as breakfast, afternoon tea and dinner.

Before School Care

During before school care children will be able to have a fresh yummy nutritous buffet breakfast including foods such as cereal, toast, fruit salad, bacon & eggs and much more. Children will also have the opportunity to engage in a range of activities such as challenging educators at board games, drawing masterpieces, constructing amazing creations & making there own scrap books.

After School Care

In after school care children will be picked up from school and brought back to the centre, they will then have a delicious afternoon tea as well as having the option of dinner as an extra if they are staying a little later. Children can participate in a range of activites such as homework, art and craft, cooking, soccer & science activites. Our service will also offer optional extras such as swimming lessons, tennis lessons and tutoring.

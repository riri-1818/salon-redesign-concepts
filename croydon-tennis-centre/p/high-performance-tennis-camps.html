High Performance Tennis Camps

Our High Performance Tennis Program is aimed at developing players into tournament players.

This holiday program helps the players to enhance their game. Each player will receive demanding physical and technical training as well as matchplay and tournament play experience.

Typical day outline is:

  • 9 - 10 am: Fitness
  • 10 - 12 pm: Ball Drilling - Stroke Production
  • 12 - 1 pm: Lunch Provided
  • 1 - 2pm: Tactical and technical development
  • 2 - 3 pm: Supervised Matchplay

Cost: $525 / 5 day week or $115 / day

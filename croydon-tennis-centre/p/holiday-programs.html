Holiday Programs

At Croydon Tennis Centre we offer a variety of #schoolholidayprograms and #kidsholidayactivities including our very successful #HolidaySports&AcademicCamp, #PREP Gross Motor Skills tennis camp, #CTCTennisHotShots JuniorDevelopment #tennis camps, #multiskills&fitnesscamps and #HolidayIntensiveSwimming programs catering for kids from the age of 3 and up. All #VacationCare and programs single day registration along with full week registration.

A sausage sizzle is provided daily for ALL full day attendants, and a supervised refreshing swim available daily for competent swimmers (swim cap and signed waiver required).

We are fully affiliated with Tennis NSW/Tennis Australia and our coaches are fully accredited and qualified, have their National Police check, WWCC, Full first aid HLTAID001, HLTAID002, HLTAID003, HLTAID004 and have completed their Identifying children at risk of significant harm course.

Parents, we are an electronic device free service, so please do not pack ipads, Nintendo Switch or any other electronic device for your children. If they have a mobile phone, we ask that at sign in these be handed to the staff member with their name, these will be kept in the office until the end of day and will be returned. We thank you for your cooperation with our policy.

Kids Party Bookings

Make your child’s birthday unforgettable with a Tennis Birthday Party at Croydon Tennis Centre!

Perfect for kids of all ages, our parties are fun, active, and tailored to create lasting memories.

Our friendly team will work with you to design a party that’s exciting and stress-free.

Here’s what’s included:

Party Package

  • Delicious birthday cake with candles (optional)
  • Lolly bags for all guests
  • 1½ hours of tennis fun - games, activities, and competitions led by a Tennis Australia Qualified Junior Development Coach
  • Racquets provided
  • Prizes and certificates for every child

Location

Croydon Tennis Centre

43 Brighton Street, Croydon NSW 2132

Pricing

  • $310 per party for up to 10 children (including the birthday child)

Additional children: $20 each

  • $370 per party for up to 10 children (including the birthday child) with cake included

Bookings are subject to date availability.

Please email Fee at info@croydontenniscentre.com.au for more information.

The EXPLORE tennis hot shots program is for juniors who are generally entering the world of tennis for the first time. Through one weekly tennis coaching lesson, we expect the vast majority of our students to gain a basic level of competency over a two year period. (Two weekly lesson options are available.)

This level of competency includes fluency in the technical fundamentals of tennis. Irrespective of their age and type of low compression ball they train with, two years of tennis lessons, once a week, will provide your child with the tools required to start playing tennis socially in their teenage and adult years at the intermediate level.

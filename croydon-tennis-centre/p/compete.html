Playing matches or taking part in some form of tennis competition is a huge part of your childs junior development pathway.

There are many tennis program participants attending years of tennis coaching, yet they have never played a match in a competition or tournament, hence never really understanding what tennis is about.

Just imagine playing soccer without having a game on the weekend and kicking some goals...(not that exciting, is it?)

Tennis is practiced to enhance the ability to perform in a match!

Competition assists in building the interest of participation and the desire to improve as well as it helping with building confidence in young individuals.

There are many ways of taking part in some form of competition, so give it a go!

Gross Motor Skills PREP Tennis Camp - 3 to 5 yo.

Our Gross Motor Skill School Holiday Program is specifically designed for 3 to 5 year old children.

Now containing componenets from our New Program Toddler's & Pre-Schoolers. This is our new exciting program focused on development of the brain through movement and physical activity.

To accomplish the basic task of 'wiring up' the brain and the body, five movement patterns should be learnt by children so they develop an understanding of opening and closing / top and bottom / front and back / left and right / both sides of the body working independently.

Other Skills to be improved:

  • Gross Locomotor Skills
  • Throwing and Catching
  • Perception

  • Co-ordination

    HICAPS

    Health fund rebates available

    At Nadeshiko Japanese Massage, we offer Remedial Massage treatments that may be eligible for rebates through your private health insurance (Health Fund) in Australia.

    Eligible services for HICAPS rebates

    To claim, you must be covered by a valid health fund. In some cases, a referral or treatment plan from a healthcare provider may be required, depending on your insurer and policy.

    For some private health funds, on-the-spot claiming is available through our HICAPS terminal. This allows you to claim your rebate immediately after your treatment, so you only need to pay the gap amount (the remaining balance after the rebate).

    Please feel free to contact us for more information about eligibility and claiming procedures.

    We are partnered with 36 major private health funds.

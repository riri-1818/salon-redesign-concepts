      Story

      Ayaluna Nails is a Sydney-based nail studio founded by an experienced nail artist with over 15 years in the industry.

      Specialising in high-quality Japanese gel, we focus on delivering long-lasting, natural-looking results while maintaining the health of your natural nails. We are particularly passionate about builder gel techniques to help strengthen and grow your nails beautifully over time.

      From clean, elegant designs to detailed hand-painted nail art, every set is carefully customised to suit each client’s style and lifestyle.

      At Ayaluna Nails, our goal is to create not only beautiful nails, but also a relaxing and personalised experience for every client.

    Ways to learn

    Six ways to fit piano into the week.

    1. One-on-one lesson

      Classical, contemporary and more. Learn for leisure while still making good progress, or prepare for exams.

    2. Small group lesson

      Two, or at most three, learning together. Young beginners build foundations; adult beginners enjoy playing duets.

    3. Lesson at your home

      Subject to availability. An option for busy parents with tight schedules.

    4. Online lesson

      On Zoom, Skype or Messenger. Suits busy adults who need a flexible schedule.

    5. Practice lesson

      A supplementary session during the week, to help little ones practise between their regular lessons.

    6. Piano practice room

      Room hire for students who do not yet have a piano at home.

    Good to know

    Our philosophy, and the practical bits.

    We believe that learning can only be fostered from a feeling of mutual respect and compassion, and we maintain those values in everything we do.
    Styles
    Classical, contemporary, pop or jazz: the music you enjoy playing.
    Exams
    Preparation for AMEB or Trinity, if you want it.
    Payment
    Bank transfer in advance, per lesson. Fees are not charged by term.
    Cancellations
    24 hours’ notice, please, unless it is an emergency. With notice, lessons can be rescheduled.

N° 01

The artist

Eva is a professional makeup artist with a diploma from a European makeup academy. Highly skilled and experienced, she is recognised for her refined technique and a distinctive approach to beauty.

Her work spans fashion and editorial makeup. She is a former Dior makeup artist, and her work has been published in Harper’s Bazaar.

Her signature style is an elegant European aesthetic. Rather than following fleeting trends, Eva focuses on delivering a strong, perfected foundation.

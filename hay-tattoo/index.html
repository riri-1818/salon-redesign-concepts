    How do I make a booking?
    You can make a booking by emailing the shop (haytattoo.syd@gmail.com) or sending a direct message via Instagram (@hay_tattoo).
    Make sure to specify the size, placement and design of your new tattoo. Include any reference photos you may have as inspiration.
    
    
    Do I need a deposit for the booking?
    Yes, to make a booking a deposit is required to hold your session time. Deposits are non-refundable and non-transferable.
    
    
    How much do tattoos cost?
    Our minimum charge for a tattoo is $120.
    Tattoos vary in price depending on the size and detail, but you can ask the artist for a rough estimate of the price prior to the appointment. In order to get a quote make sure to specify the size, placement and design of your new tattoo. Include any reference photos you may have as inspiration.
    
    
    How to care for your new tattoo?
    Care instructions vary from artist to artist so make sure you follow the advice given specifically to you.
    General care includes the application of anti-septic cream at least 3 times daily for 2–3 weeks, avoid alcohol for 2 days after and avoid swimming for 2 weeks post tattoo.
    
    
    About the payment.
    We are cash only, there are ATMs nearby for your convenience.

    Important note

    At Nails by Miho, we pride ourselves in having impeccable hygiene practices. This way, we keep all of our clients safe from unwanted infections. For this reason, we do not provide services to clients with active fungal infections. If you suffer from a fungal infection and would like to have your nails cared for by us, we recommend that prior to engaging us for treatment you seek the help of Bespoke Health and Performance, our preferred podiatry partners, to eliminate your fungal infection. This way we keep not only you, but all of our clients safe from unwanted infections.

    Our Japanese nail system of choice

    Ladiposh

    Ladiposh presents an exquisite soft gel nail system originating from Japan, renowned for its breathable, enduring composition. It stands as our esteemed recommendation to our clientele.

    Esteemed for its versatility, Ladiposh enables an infinite array of designs and nail art, prioritising the health of the nail above all.

    Remarkably safe and effortless to remove, this system negates the necessity for UV exposure, ensuring a premier and health-conscious nail care experience.

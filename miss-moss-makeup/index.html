Welcome to Miss Moss Makeup

Welcome to Miss Moss Makeup — professional makeup and hair Sydney services for weddings, events, formals, photoshoots, corporate events, and special occasions across Sydney.

I’m Greer, the owner and artist behind Miss Moss Makeup, with over 18 years of experience as a professional Sydney makeup artist and hairstylist. I specialise in modern, polished makeup and effortless hair styling that enhances your natural features while still feeling like you.

Whether you’re looking for soft glam makeup, bridal hair and makeup, event styling, or natural radiant beauty looks, my approach is always tailored, timeless, and designed to photograph beautifully in both natural light and professional photography.

Miss Moss Makeup provides mobile makeup and hair services across Sydney, travelling to homes, hotels, wedding venues, and photoshoot locations for a relaxed and seamless experience. I also welcome clients to my private Alexandria studio for bridal trials, special occasion appointments, and personalised consultations.

“Unlike larger makeup agencies, when you book Miss Moss Makeup, you work directly with an experienced artist who genuinely cares about achieving the perfect look for you.”

As a highly experienced Sydney makeup and hair artist, I understand how important it is to feel confident, comfortable, and looked after throughout the entire process. From your first enquiry through to your appointment, every booking is handled personally by me to ensure a consistent, professional, and high-quality experience.

Unlike larger makeup agencies, when you book Miss Moss Makeup, you work directly with an experienced artist who genuinely cares about achieving the perfect look for you. My style is known for fresh skin, soft glam makeup, long-lasting finishes, and beautifully styled hair that feels elevated without ever looking overdone.

Based in Sydney and servicing surrounding areas, Miss Moss Makeup is trusted by brides, clients, and photographers for professional, reliable, and beautifully natural makeup and hair artistry.

If you’re searching for experienced, luxury makeup and hair in Sydney, I’d love to help you feel confident, radiant, and your absolute best for your special event.

I can’t wait to meet you.

      How to prepare for your appointment

      To ensure best possible results, please follow the preparation guidelines below.

      1. Hair preparation

        Please arrive with your hair freshly washed and dry. A rough blow-dry or air-dry is perfect – please avoid using hair straighteners.

        If you have booked a blow wave, kindly arrive with freshly washed, damp hair.

      2. Skin preparation

        You may use your usual skincare products to your appointment.

        I will apply serum, moisturiser and primer to properly prepare your skin before the makeup application.

      3. Inspiration photos

        You are welcome to bring inspiration photos to your appointment or email them through beforehand. This helps ensure we achieve your desired look.

      4. Appointment duration

        Please allow approximately 1.5 – 2 hours for combined makeup and hair.

        For hair only or makeup only appointments please allow 45 mins – 1 hour.

      5. Payment

        Holding deposits are required for group bookings and to secure wedding dates.

        Full payment is required on the day of your appointment. Payment can be made via cash or card (please note a 2% surcharge applies to card payments).

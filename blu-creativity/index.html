Blu Creativity is a Japanese hair salon in Sydney CBD, established in 2002. Our experienced Japanese hairdressers specialise in women’s and men’s haircuts, hair colour, Japanese hair straightening, balayage and perms.

blu:/ creativity is a fully serviced Japanese hair salon offering a total hair and beauty experience. We invite you to experience the difference and enjoy stylish fashionable hair in the heart of Sydney.

Men’s & women’s haircuts

Discover professional men’s and women’s haircuts at Blu Creativity, a Japanese hair salon in Sydney CBD. Our experienced Japanese hairdressers specialise in precision cutting, modern hairstyles, classic cuts and personalised styling to suit your hair type, face shape and lifestyle.

Recognised by Urban List as one of Sydney CBD’s best hairdressers, Blu Creativity is dedicated to providing quality haircuts and personalised service. Whether you’re looking for a stylish men’s haircut, a fresh women’s hairstyle or a complete new look, our team is here to help.

Professional hair colouring

Our experienced Japanese hair colourists offer personalised hair colouring services, including natural-looking colours, grey hair coverage, highlights and balayage. We use professional hair colour products from trusted brands such as Wella, Schwarzkopf and Shiseido to help achieve beautiful, long-lasting colour results.

Whether you prefer a subtle colour refresh or a bold new look, our team will help you find a hair colour that complements your skin tone, hairstyle and personal style.

Highlights & foil colouring

Our experienced Japanese hair colourists offer customised foil colouring techniques, including highlights, lowlights, balayage and ombré, to create natural-looking dimension, brightness and depth. Whether you’re looking for subtle highlights, a sun-kissed balayage effect or a more dramatic colour transformation, we tailor each service to complement your hair type, skin tone and personal style.

Japanese digital perm & hair perm

Our experienced Japanese hairstylists specialise in creating beautiful, natural-looking curls, soft waves and hairstyles tailored to your hair type and personal style. Japanese digital perm is an advanced perm technique that uses temperature-controlled heated rods to help create soft, defined curls and waves with long-lasting results.

Whether you’re looking for natural waves, added volume or a complete hairstyle transformation, our team will recommend a suitable perm technique based on your hair condition, length and desired look.

Japanese hair treatments & repair

Our specialised hair treatments are designed to nourish dry, damaged and chemically treated hair, helping to improve moisture retention, smoothness and overall hair appearance. Using professional Japanese hair care products containing ingredients such as keratin, collagen and CMC (Cell Membrane Complex), our treatments help condition the hair, reduce frizz and leave it feeling softer, smoother and more manageable.

Whether your hair has been affected by colouring, heat styling or everyday environmental stress, our experienced Japanese hairstylists will recommend a suitable hair treatment based on your hair type and condition.

    Japanese hair straightening

    blu :/ creativity specialises in hair straightening and is the industry leading expert in the service and training of the latest Hair Straightening services. Typically the service is called Japanese Hair Straightening. blu :/ creativity brings you THE BEST permanent hair straightening on the market and latest technology and products imported from Japan.

    Our experienced Japanese hairstylists specialise in Japanese hair straightening, also known as thermal reconditioning, to help transform frizzy, wavy or curly hair into smooth, straight and manageable hair. We use professional Japanese hair straightening products from Shiseido Professional, a renowned Japanese beauty brand known for its advanced hair care technology and high-quality formulations.

    Each treatment is carefully tailored to your hair type and condition, helping to achieve smooth, sleek and long-lasting results while prioritising the health of your hair.

No one has a perfectly balanced body and mind.

At AMANO Remedial Massage, I don’t just focus on pain or injuries. No one has a perfectly balanced body and mind, and even small imbalances can develop into bigger problems over time. But if we work together, we may be able to prevent them before they start. I aim to help your body recover, move more freely, and stay in balance for the long term.

The AMANO Method is an innovative and unique style of Remedial Massage that combines the knowledge and techniques of both Western and Eastern practices. Pressure is adjusted from gentle (superficial) to firm (deep), according to your comfort level.

For clients seeking treatment for pain or imbalance, a Range Of Motion assessment will be included to help identify areas of restriction. The treatment may also incorporate techniques such as Soft Tissue Release, Myo-Fascial Release, Japanese Shiatsu, and Trigger Point therapy to relieve tension, improve mobility, enhance posture, and promote healthy circulation.

The AMANO Method aims to bring the body into better balance and support overall physical and mental well-being.

Where it may help. Some examples.

Treatment principles

Acute stage0–3 weeks
Use R.I.C.E (Rest, Ice, Compression, Elevation) immediately. See a General Practitioner (GP) as soon as possible for further advice and treatment.
Subacute stage
The period after the initial injury, usually 4–6 weeks.
Chronic stage
Develops slowly over time, with gradual changes in the body, such as muscles tightening, joints becoming stiff, or tissues weakening.

Shizuka Michelle AMANO

I began my career as a beauty therapist in 2002, where I realised that true wellness comes not only from the outside but also from within the body.

This insight led me to study Eastern medicine and practise acupuncture in Japan, where I also gained experience working in a clinical setting. These experiences deepened my understanding of the connection between the body and mind, and the importance of holistic care.

After moving to Australia, I completed a Diploma of Remedial Massage and developed my own treatment style, blending Eastern medicine with Western remedial techniques. I currently offer Remedial Massage and Dry Needling in Sydney, providing individualised care to support each client’s unique needs and overall well-being.

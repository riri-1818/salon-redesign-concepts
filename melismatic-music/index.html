Teachers

Performers who teach.

Head teacher and director

Maya Stempien

Maya established Melismatic Music in January 2017. She was classically trained from the age of 12, completed her AMEB exams with an A+ in Advanced Contemporary Singing, and holds a Diploma of Music.

She has performed in musical theatre, bands and festivals, sings at weddings across Sydney as “Maya & David Duet”, releases her own R&B and pop as “Maya Flora”, and conducts choirs with the NSW Federation of Community Language Schools.

Substitute teacher and MC

David Miller

David plays acoustic and electric guitar, sings and plays piano, and performs most weekends as a soloist, in a duet and with a band. He also works with “Outloud” at Bankstown Arts Centre, mentoring young people in the arts.

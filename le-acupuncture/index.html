About Hanh Le

Trained in Sydney, Chengdu and Vietnam.

Hanh Le is a fully qualified and registered Doctor of Chinese Medicine. She completed a Health Science degree in Traditional Chinese Medicine at the University of Technology Sydney, then went on to internships in Chengdu and Vietnam, to observe traditional medicine where it originates.

Hanh believes in integrating evidence-based protocols from Chinese and Western medicine.

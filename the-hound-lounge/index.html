Start with a Taster Session

A typical full day includes supervised structured play, mental activities and rest time.

We understand that all dogs are unique, some need more rest and alone time, whilst others prefer to be in amongst the crowd and romping around. A member of staff will always be on hand supervising your dog to ensure we are meeting your dog’s needs.

New to The Hound Lounge? Three steps.

We do not take new bookings over the phone. You will not be able to make a booking yourself until your dog has attended a Taster Session.

  1. 1

    Register

    Please have a copy of your dog’s vaccination certificate ready to upload (your vet can issue a computer generated certificate if you do not have one). This will be checked manually and you will receive an email from us to book a Taster Session.

  2. 2

    Taster Session

    All new dogs attend a 3 hour Taster Session ($35) prior to making a full/half day booking. During this time we will assess your dog to ensure our daycare is suitable, for the safety and happiness of all dogs in our care.

  3. 3

    Book your days

    After the Taster Session, log in to the client portal to make a booking.

Our facility

Our brand new, specially designed facility is one of the largest in Sydney and is sure to provide your dog with the perfect daycare experience every single time they visit.

Our spacious building is separated into different areas where our perfectly matched groups of dogs can enjoy their visit, ensuring that each dog has a good experience and never has to feel overwhelmed by the other dogs – they’ll be begging to come back!

We may be biased, but we have one of the best facilities around. Still not sure? Contact us to arrange a tour of the building, we are more than happy to show you around to give you that all important peace of mind.

We understand that your dogs are part of your family and we give them the same care and attention that we provide for our own animals.

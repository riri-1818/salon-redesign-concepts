05 — About us

Japanese technique, in the heart of Chatswood.

  1. Since 2012

    A Japanese-style salon bringing the styles that are popular in Japan to Sydney.

  2. One stylist, start to finish

    Colour, shampoo and blow-dry are all done by the same hairdresser.

  3. English and Japanese

    An English–Japanese translator is available, so nothing gets lost at the consultation.

  4. Giving back

    Since 2013 the salon has sponsored a child in Nepal through World Vision, and donates regularly to Child Rescue and the Cerebral Palsy Alliance.
